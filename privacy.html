Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Replaces the June 19, 2026 policy; the change is the "Anonymous usage statistics" section below.

Cold Case Bureau is built and operated by Thomas Masters. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

No personal information. The app does not collect, transmit, or store personal information about you on any server. There are no accounts, no logins, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. From version 2.1.3 the app sends anonymous usage statistics, described in the next section.

Anonymous usage statistics

From version 2.1.3, the app counts a few things so we can tell which parts of the game are working: when a case file is opened, when a case is closed (with the case, the number of theories filed and whether it was today's Daily, a season file or an archive case), and when the Daily result is shared. These counts are sent to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. Before anything leaves your phone, the device identifier is combined with a secret and hashed, so neither we nor TelemetryDeck can identify you or link you across apps; no name, email, contact, location or IP address is stored. TelemetryDeck's own handling of the data is described in its privacy policy. This is declared on the App Store as "Product Interaction" and "Device ID", not linked to you and not used for tracking.

What stays on your device

Your reputation score, tier, solved cases, and active investigation sessions are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage frameworks. This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app or reset your progress in Settings, the data is removed.

If you choose to send us feedback

The in-app feedback button and the Settings feedback option open Apple's standard Mail composer with a pre-filled message that includes the case ID (for case-specific feedback), the app version, your iOS version, and your device model. The message is only sent if you choose to send it, and it goes directly to thomasmasters@gmail.com. We use your message to fix the bug or answer your question. We do not share it with anyone.

Apple's analytics

If you have App Analytics enabled in iOS Settings, Apple may collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports from apps including this one. That data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can opt out in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Cold Case Bureau does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The anonymous usage statistics above cannot identify a person of any age.

Changes to this policy

If anything material changes, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will be revised.
